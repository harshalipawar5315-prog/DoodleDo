# 🚀 Walkthrough: DoodleDo × VibeRoom

The Doodledo app has been completely redesigned into a high-end productivity dashboard.

## 📺 New Layout
The app now uses a 3-column grid for maximum efficiency:
1.  **Left Sidebar (Vibes)**: Choose your atmosphere. Each vibe changes the background animation, accent colors, and ambient music.
2.  **Center (Focus)**: Contains the clock, a daily wisdom quote, and the **Pomodoro Workstation**.
3.  **Right (Planner)**: Manage your Tasks, view your daily Schedule, and check your Productivity Log.

## 🌧️ Immersive Features
- **Animated Canvas**: Uses high-performance particle systems to create rain, fireflies, and party glows.
- **Ambient Sound Engine**: Generates real-time audio (Rain, Lofi, Ambient) without needing external files.
- **Music Bar**: Control your background sounds and track progress at the bottom.

## 📈 Productivity & Gamification
- **XP System**: Earn XP for completing Pomodoros and finishing tasks.
- **Streak Tracker**: Keep your daily momentum going.
- **Task Pips**: See exactly how many sessions you've completed for each task.

## 🛠️ Technical Improvements
- **LocalStorage Persistence**: All tasks and stats are saved locally.
- **Glassmorphism UI**: Heavy use of `backdrop-filter` and translucent layers for a premium feel.
- **Responsive Design**: Adapts smoothly to different screen sizes.
